Questions

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 14 or later. On first launch, Squint helps you install Ollama and downloads Gemma 3 (about 3.3 GB, once). Apple Silicon is recommended; Intel works but is slower.

Does Gemma see my screenshot?

No. Apple's on-device OCR reads the text, pattern rules find candidates, and Gemma only sees each candidate with a line of context, to decide whether it's private. It never sees the image, and it never decides where to draw a box.

What if Gemma isn't running?

Squint fails safe: anything uncertain is hidden. The worst case is hiding too much, never leaking.

Will it change my original screenshot?

Never. The redacted copy goes to your clipboard and is saved next to the original as name.redacted.png.

Why is the floating screenshot thumbnail gone?

It delays saving by a few seconds, so Squint turns it off while running and restores your setting when it quits.
